Our History

Qiji was founded in 2019 to support the evolving needs of the FPSO ecosystem, initially focusing on regulatory consultancy. Since then, we have supported the approval, startup, and operation of multiple offshore assets before the Brazilian authorities. Qiji has expanded into other regulatory-related engineering services for onshore and offshore facilities.
